Biscuit

Description

This apple cinnamon variation on popular butter swim biscuits is chock-full of warm apple goodness. Lightly sweet, super soft, and fluffy, they stay moist for days and are hard to beat with a cup of coffee or tea. Use any apple you like, such as Granny Smith, and peel it or leave the skin on—whichever you prefer.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 2 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup firmly packed brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 2 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon, divided
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1 1/2 cups diced apple
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup white sugar

Steps

  1. Place butter into an 8x8-inch square pan. Place pan into the oven, and turn the oven on to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C). Melt butter as oven preheats for a few minutes, then remove pan from the oven, and leave the oven on to continue preheating.
  2. Whisk together flour, brown sugar, baking powder, 1 teaspoon cinnamon, salt, baking soda, and nutmeg in a large bowl until combined. Add diced apple and toss until incorporated. Add buttermilk and vanilla and mix until no dry clumps of flour remain (batter will be somewhat loose). Pour batter over the melted butter in the pan, and use a spatula to spread it out slightly. Use a sharp knife to cut batter into 9 sections.
  3. In a small bowl, mix together white sugar and remaining 1 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon. Sprinkle this mixture evenly over the top of the batter. Place pan onto a baking sheet to catch any potential spills.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out mostly clean, with only a few crumbs and no wet batter, 32 to 38 minutes. Allow biscuits to cool for about 30 minutes before serving. Can be served warm or at room temperature.
